About Us

We wear the mask so the truth can speak.

We are MrTeluguMan. Not one man — a mask worn by many. Behind it stand ordinary Telugu people who looked at the silence, the fear, and the fixed headlines, and said one word: enough.

Why we exist

Power protects power. Newspapers look away. Channels change the subject. And the people who pay the price are always the ones without a microphone. We picked up that microphone — and we are not putting it down.

We belong to no party. We are bought by no one. We take one side only: the truth. When someone abuses their position — in a government office, a police station, a boardroom — we ask the question everyone else is too afraid to ask, and we do not stop asking until it is answered.

They do not fear a name. They fear the truth. So we hide the name — and let the truth walk in loud.

What we cover

Our work runs across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and all of India — the corruption that never reached the front page, the injustice that was quietly settled, the story buried under the official story.

Some of it is investigation. Some of it is simply saying out loud what a whole state already whispers. Every word is checked before we publish, because the truth is the only weapon we carry — and we will not blunt it with a single lie.

Stand with us

This is where you come in.

The powerful are counting on one thing: that you will stay quiet. That you will see it, shake your head, and scroll on. Every time you do, they win.

So don’t.

You have seen something. You are holding proof no one would print. You are the story we are looking for. Send it to us — with your name or without it, the choice is always yours, and it is always yours to keep.

Follow us. Share what we publish. One share is a whisper. A thousand shares is a roar they cannot silence.

We are not asking you to be brave. We are asking you to stop being silent.
