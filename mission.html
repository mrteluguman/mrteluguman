Mission & Vision

Vision

An uncompromising vision of the future.

Mission

It’s easy sometimes to feel like you’re powerless when you come out in the streets and you march and yell and nobody hears you — but we are here to tell you today:

You are powerful.

Our mission is to enlighten people with true knowledge — the facts, and the truth behind them — so that no one is left in the dark, and no one is easy to mislead.

We believe a people armed with knowledge, free to think for themselves, can never be ruled by lies.
